# Satisfactory planner guide

Use only on a private/self-hosted or dedicated co-op save; Satisfactory has no
anti-cheat, but automation should stay limited to worlds the owner controls.
Observe health, held equipment, nearby hazards (fall damage, gas, wildlife)
and the current build/dismantle target before acting, and prioritize safe
pathing over factory floors and conveyors rather than shortcuts through
hazards. Never repeat build, movement or resource-gathering intents during
loading, death or open menus.
