# Raft planner guide

Use only on a private/self-hosted server; Raft has no anti-cheat but
automation should stay limited to worlds the owner controls. Observe health,
hunger, thirst, the current hotbar item and nearby sharks or hostile islanders
before acting, and prioritize retrieving floating debris with the hook, keeping
the raft's core systems (purifier, battery, foundations) intact, and staying
aboard rather than swimming far from the raft. Never repeat movement or
gathering intents during loading, death or open menus.
