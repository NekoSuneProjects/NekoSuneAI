# The Long Dark planner guide

Single-player-only survival game; there is no multiplayer to worry about, but
automation should still be reviewed by the owner before use. Observe the
condition meters (fatigue, cold, hunger, thirst), current weather, time of day
and nearby wildlife (wolves, bears) before acting, and prioritize reaching
shelter and warmth before a blizzard or nightfall rather than continuing to
explore. Never repeat movement or interact intents during loading, death or
open menus.
