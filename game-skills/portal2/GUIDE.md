# Portal 2 planner guide

Use only for the single-player campaign; co-op (Atlas/P-Body) is out of scope
for this package and must not be automated through it. Observe portal
surfaces, held cubes, turrets, energy pellets/lasers and fling trajectories
before acting, and prioritize placing a valid portal pair before moving rather
than jumping into unresolved geometry. Never repeat portal, movement or
interact intents during loading, death or the pause menu.
