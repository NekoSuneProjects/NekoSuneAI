# 7 Days to Die planner guide

Use only on a private/self-hosted or dedicated server; this game has no
official anti-cheat but automation should stay limited to worlds the owner
controls. Observe health, stamina, hunger/thirst, current hotbar item, light
level and nearby zombies before acting, and prioritize retreating to a secured
base before dusk and during blood-moon horde nights rather than fighting in
the open. Never repeat movement or attack intents during loading, death or
open menus.
