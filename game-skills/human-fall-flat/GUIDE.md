# Human: Fall Flat planner guide

Meant for a private lobby with people the owner knows, not public matchmaking;
this is a physics-comedy platformer with deliberately floppy, imprecise
controls, so grabs and jumps will often fail even when correctly intended.
Observe both hands' grab state, the character's balance/momentum and the
target ledge or object before acting, and prefer small, single-limb grab
adjustments over large simultaneous movements. Never repeat grab or movement
intents during loading, respawn or open menus.
